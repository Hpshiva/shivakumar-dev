    [ 07 ] Questions

    Things people usually ask.

    Something else? Just ask me.

    What technologies do you use?

    Whatever fits your project best. I specialise in modern JavaScript and TypeScript: React, Next.js and Node.js. I'm comfortable across backends, cloud platforms and databases when a project needs more than a frontend.

    How long does a project take?

    A marketing website typically takes 2–4 weeks. A full web application with dashboards and a backend can take 6–12 weeks. You'll see real progress at every milestone, not just at the end.

    Do you design too, or only develop?

    Both. My core is engineering, but I have a strong eye for UI and UX. I'm happy to work from your designer's Figma files or shape the design with you from scratch.

    What happens after launch?

    You get documentation, full access to the codebase, and 14 days of post-launch support for anything urgent. If you want ongoing help with new features and scaling, I offer hourly support with no minimum commitment.
